Is pepperoni halal?

Pepperoni is the most popular pizza topping in the US — and one of the easiest places to miss pork, especially on pizza, snack packs and lunch kits.

Updated October 18, 2026

Short answerRegular pepperoni is not halal — it’s usually made from pork, or pork and beef. Beef or turkey pepperoni avoids pork, and halal-certified pepperoni is also made from halal-slaughtered meat.

What pepperoni is made of

Traditional pepperoni is a cured sausage of pork, or a pork and beef blend. The same goes for most salami and chorizo; prosciutto, pancetta and guanciale are always pork.

Watch out for

  • Pizza and frozen meals — “pepperoni” on the box usually means pork.
  • Casings — some sausages use pork casings even when the meat isn’t pork.
  • Snack packs and lunch kits with salami or pepperoni.

Frequently asked questions

Is turkey pepperoni halal?

Turkey pepperoni doesn’t contain pork, so it’s acceptable for many Muslims. If you only eat dhabiha meat, look for halal-certified turkey pepperoni.

Is beef pepperoni halal?

It avoids pork. It’s fully halal when the beef was slaughtered Islamically — look for halal certification.

Is salami halal?

Most salami contains pork. Beef or turkey salami avoids pork; halal-certified salami is also made from halal-slaughtered meat.

This guide is general information, not a religious ruling. Scholars sometimes differ — when in doubt, consult a qualified scholar or a certified halal authority.
