Is whey protein halal?

Whey is in protein powders, bars, baked goods and snacks. It comes from milk, but it’s made during cheese production — which is where the halal question comes in.

Updated October 18, 2026

Short answerWhey made with microbial or vegetable rennet — or with no rennet at all — is halal. When the cheese was made with animal rennet from an animal not slaughtered Islamically, many scholars consider the whey doubtful, while others (notably the Hanafi view) accept it. Halal-certified whey settles the question.

Where whey comes from

When milk is turned into cheese, it separates into solid curds (the cheese) and liquid whey. Whey protein powder is made by filtering and drying that liquid. The milk itself is halal — the question is the rennet used to make the cheese.

Is whey halal?

SituationStatus
Halal-certified whey or protein powderHalal
Whey from cheese made with microbial / vegetable rennetHalal
Acid whey (from yogurt or cottage cheese without rennet)Halal
Whey with unknown rennetMushbooh scholars differ

Other ingredients in protein powders

  • Gelatin in some protein bars and capsules — check the source.
  • Natural flavors — doubtful without certification.
  • Collagen — from cattle, pigs or fish; check the source.

Frequently asked questions

Is whey protein isolate halal?

Isolate is a more filtered form of whey, so the same question applies: it’s halal when the rennet used was microbial or halal, or the product is halal-certified.

Can Muslims drink whey protein?

Many do, especially halal-certified brands or under the view that accepts animal rennet. If you follow a stricter view, choose halal-certified whey.

Is whey vegetarian?

Whey is vegetarian only when it comes from cheese made with microbial or vegetable rennet.

This guide is general information, not a religious ruling. Scholars sometimes differ — when in doubt, consult a qualified scholar or a certified halal authority.
